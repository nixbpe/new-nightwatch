## Design decisions

- Preserve the vertical fix: wrapper owns `border-b`, nav owns `-mb-px`; do not mask vertical overflow or truncate labels. Settings wrap entire fixed-height tabs, not text inside them; no icon-only or new keyboard model.
- MFA connectors are decorative and hidden in the vertical layout. On narrow screens the footer stacks secondary before primary in DOM order; at `sm` it returns to a row. Member tables remain unchanged; only tables may scroll horizontally (LAY-02).
